What is the DXP?

The Data Exchange Platform (DXP) is the government-wide layer that lets Abu Dhabi entities share data with each other securely and on agreed terms. Instead of one-off requests and email attachments, every entity publishes the data it chooses to share to a single platform — so you can find and subscribe to data published from DCD as well as other entities, with an approval flow for each subscription request to data products. DCD is connected to the DXP, but your account doesn't have access yet — request access above to browse the catalogue and subscribe to data.
