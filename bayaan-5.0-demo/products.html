Bayaan Products offer a diverse array of functionalities to support effective strategic planning and decision-making. Key among these is the role of scenario drivers, which serve as pivotal variables influencing the formulation of scenarios. These drivers encompass a broad spectrum of factors, ranging from economic trajectories and technological advancements to transformative social shifts. Complementing this is the remarkable feature of Insights Discovery, an integral component of Bayaan, which adeptly uncovers latent patterns within data. By revealing these concealed trends, decision-makers are empowered to identify emerging developments and foster valuable insights. The inclusion of Quick Insights further enhances the utility of Bayaan, promptly delivering actionable insights that can drive prompt and informed decisions. Moreover, the incorporation of Geospatial Maps enriches the analytical process by offering visual representations of data on a geographical scale. This feature facilitates an enhanced understanding of regional trends, consequently facilitating well-informed decisions rooted in comprehensive insights.

Analytical Apps

Scenario drivers are the key factors and variables that shape and influence the development of different scenarios in strategic planning. They encompass variables such as economic trends, technological advancements, market dynamics, regulatory changes, and social shifts, which are used to create a range of possible future scenarios. These drivers provide the context and foundation for scenario planning.

Insights Discovery is an integral component of Bayaan, which adeptly uncovers latent patterns within data. By revealing these concealed trends, decision-makers are empowered to identify emerging developments and foster valuable insights across every domain.

Bayaan leverages statistical, big, and open data to provide interactive dashboards, models, and simulations for decision-makers, enabling policy impact assessment and resource optimization.

Official Indicators

Official Indicators are the most reliable and up-to-date source of statistical information. These indicators are updated on a regular basis and provide valuable insights into the current state of the economy.

Experimental indicators

The Experimental Indicators within Bayaan introduces novel metrics and measurements that go beyond traditional data points. These indicators provide fresh insights into complex economic and societal aspects, enabling decision-makers to gain a deeper understanding of emerging trends, anticipate future developments, and make informed choices.

Reports

"Bayaan Reports" is a comprehensive feature that generates in-depth analyses and visual summaries based on the integrated data. These reports offer valuable insights, trends, and simulations, aiding decision-makers in policy formulation, resource allocation, and strategy development.
