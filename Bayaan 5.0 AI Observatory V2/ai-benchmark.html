Benchmark figures combine Abu Dhabi's 2026 SCAD Enterprise and Individual AI Adoption Surveys with seven independent global AI indices and Eurostat/OECD 2025 figures for peer economies. Definitions, survey years and methodologies differ across sources — cross-comparisons are directional, not exact. GCC country-level AI adoption figures are not yet published and are not included here. Expand for source details.

Enterprise adoption
SCAD Enterprise AI Adoption Survey, 2026 — 4,189 of 5,160 enterprises surveyed (81% response); benchmarked against Eurostat/OECD 2025 national enterprise-AI-use figures.
Individual adoption
SCAD Individual AI Adoption Survey, 2026 — 15,042 respondents aged 15+, weighted to 2,540,268 residents. Not part of the cross-country benchmark shown here.
AI diffusion
Microsoft AI Economy Institute, Global AI Diffusion Index, Q1 2026 — 147 countries.
AI vibrancy
Stanford HAI, AI Index Report 2025 — 36 economies.
Skills investment
Oliver Wyman Industry 5.0, 2025 — 92 countries.
Gov. AI readiness
Oxford Insights, Government AI Readiness Index, 2025 — 195 countries.
Intelligent cities
BCG, 2025 — 61 cities; Abu Dhabi is ranked at the city level, not the UAE.
Usage intensity
Anthropic Economic Index, 2025 — 121 countries.
Innovation outputs
WIPO Global Innovation Index, 2025 — 139 economies.
