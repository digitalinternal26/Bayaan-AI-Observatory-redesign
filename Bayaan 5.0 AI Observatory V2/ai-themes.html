AI in the Economy

73% of Abu Dhabi enterprises have adopted AI — concentrated in everyday operations, and unevenly spread across sectors.

Purpose of AI use · share of enterprises
Administration26%
Production / service processes23%
Marketing or sales22%
Finance management13%
R&D or innovation2%

Sector adoption ranges from 66% (Other Service Activities) to 86% (Electricity). SCAD AI Enterprise Adoption Survey, 2026.

Enterprise Readiness

Interest in AI is high among non-adopters, and governance is still forming among those who have already adopted.

Top barriers to adoption · % of non-adopters
Lack of relevant expertise16.7%
Incompatibility with existing systems16.1%
Costs too high13.8%

47% of non-adopters plan to adopt within 3–5 years. SCAD AI Enterprise Adoption Survey, 2026.

AI strategy & governance status · adopters
No AI strategy or policy in place33.1%
Internal AI policies or guidelines18.2%
Formal AI strategy11.1%

18.2% + 11.1% = the 29% governance headline. SCAD AI Enterprise Adoption Survey, 2026.

AI Workforce
