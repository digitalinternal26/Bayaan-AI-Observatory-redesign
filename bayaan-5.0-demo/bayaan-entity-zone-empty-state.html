Data Exchange Platform

What is the DXP?

The Data Exchange Platform (DXP) is the government-wide layer that lets Abu Dhabi entities share data with each other securely and on agreed terms. Instead of one-off requests and email attachments, every entity publishes the data it chooses to share to a single platform — so you can find and subscribe to data published from DCD as well as other entities, there will be an approval flow for the subscription request to data products. You're already connected to the DXP, which is why the catalogue below is open to you right now.
