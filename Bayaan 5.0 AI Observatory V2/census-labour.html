Executive Brief

Abu Dhabi's employed population reached 2.98 million in 2025, growing 7.7% from 2024. Non-Emiratis account for 88.8% of employment, while the workforce remains strongly male-dominated at 78.2%. White-collar employment is growing faster than blue-collar (+9.2% vs +6.6%), pointing to a gradual shift toward higher-skilled roles.

Key Insights

Abu Dhabi's employed population reached 2.98M in 2025, an increase of 7.7% from 2024.
Non-Emiratis account for 88.8% of the employed population, reflecting Abu Dhabi's reliance on an international workforce.
The employed population is strongly male-dominated (78.2% male), a pattern most pronounced among non-Emiratis (80.9% male vs Emirati 56.8% male).
White-collar employment is growing faster than blue-collar (+9.2% vs +6.6%), a gradual shift toward higher-skilled roles.
Emirati jobseekers are heavily concentrated among 20–24 year-olds (5,715 of 15,855, or 36%), consistent with the group's broader 71% female skew.
