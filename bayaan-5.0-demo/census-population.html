Executive Brief

Abu Dhabi Emirate's population reached 4,441,550 in 2025. Non-Emiratis account for 81.6% of residents, while Emiratis represent 18.4%. Population is concentrated in Abu Dhabi Region, which holds 69% of the Emirate's population, followed by Al Ain (23%) and Al Dhafra (8%). The Emirati population is close to gender balance (50.1% male / 49.9% female), while the non-Emirati population is male-skewed (71.2% male).

Key Insights

Abu Dhabi Emirate's population reached 4,441,550 in 2025, with non-Emiratis accounting for 81.6% of residents and Emiratis for 18.4%.
Population is concentrated in Abu Dhabi Region, which holds 69% (3,066,635) of the Emirate's population, followed by Al Ain (23%) and Al Dhafra (8%).
The Emirati population is close to gender balance (50.1% male / 49.9% female), while the non-Emirati population is male-skewed (71.2% male) — a notable contrast between the two groups.
Non-Emiratis make up 3,624,415 residents against 817,135 Emiratis — Abu Dhabi's population is overwhelmingly non-Emirati.
