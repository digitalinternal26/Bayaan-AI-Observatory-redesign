Abu Dhabi · 2025 Census data

Executive Brief

Abu Dhabi's population reached 4.44 million in 2025, growing 7.4% year-on-year, with the employed population up 7.7% and housing units up 2.9%. Growth remains concentrated in Abu Dhabi Region, and the population skews heavily male (67.3%) — a pattern driven by the non-Emirati workforce.

Key Insights

What do these Census findings mean?

Abu Dhabi's population reached 4.44M in 2025, growing 7.4% year-on-year and skewing 67.3% male — a pattern driven almost entirely by the non-Emirati group, while the Emirati population is close to balanced (50.1%/49.9%)
Employment reached 2.98M, increasing 7.7%, with white-collar employment growing faster than blue-collar employment — a sign of strong labour force growth
The Emirate recorded 326,130 buildings and 899,575 units in 2025, with residential units accounting for 58.0% — housing supply continues to expand
Abu Dhabi Region accounts for 69% of the population and approximately 69.4% of housing units — population and housing remain concentrated in the region
71% of Emirati jobseekers are female, contrasting with the overall male-skewed population and labour force — a distinctive gender pattern among Emirati jobseekers
