Overview

Abu Dhabi · 2025 AI Observatory data

Executive Brief

AI adoption is high across Abu Dhabi, with 73% of surveyed enterprises and 86% of individuals aged 15+ reporting AI use. Enterprise adoption is ahead of the selected international benchmarks, while the UAE ranks first globally in the separate Microsoft AI Diffusion measure. The next challenge is moving from adoption to capability and governance: only 12.4% of individuals reach advanced AI capability, and 29% of enterprises report AI governance arrangements.

Key Insights

What these findings mean, distilled into the takeaways that matter most.

Advanced AI capability sits at just 12.4% of individuals, even though 86.0% have used AI in some form — the next constraint on Abu Dhabi's AI story is depth, not access.
Programming and AI-app-development show the widest self-reported skills gap, at +26.2 percentage points between what people want to learn and what they already know.
Only 29% of adopting enterprises have a formal AI strategy or governance policy in place, even as 73% already use AI in day-to-day operations.
Just 2% of reported enterprise AI use goes toward R&D or innovation — activity is concentrated in administration, production and marketing instead.
Sentiment toward AI is already positive (71.3%) and confidence in the UAE's AI future runs even higher (88.6%) — an early advantage for closing the capability and governance gaps above.
