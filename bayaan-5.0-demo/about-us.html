What is Bayaan?

Bayaan is a new artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning tool that enables a new analytics platform designed to support policymaking and future planning within the Abu Dhabi government.

What we do?

Bayaan leverages statistical, big, and open data to provide interactive dashboards, models, and simulations for decision-makers, enabling policy impact assessment and resource optimization. It facilitates data sharing between government and private sectors, using advanced algorithms for automated collection. Through AI-driven analytics, it generates "what-if" reports and future economic simulations. With 2,800 indicators, including operating, employments, and real-estate data, it's a successful tool aiding Abu Dhabi's policymakers with informed decisions.
