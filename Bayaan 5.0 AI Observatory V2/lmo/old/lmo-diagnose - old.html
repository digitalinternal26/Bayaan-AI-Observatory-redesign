Overview

Abu Dhabi's employed population has crossed 2.9M (+10.6% since 2022) with the employment rate at 96.2% and unemployment easing to 3.8%. Non-Emirati participation dominates every age band, while degree holders show the strongest employment correlation. Emiratisation is progressing gradually as the private-sector share of Emirati employment continues its slow shift.
