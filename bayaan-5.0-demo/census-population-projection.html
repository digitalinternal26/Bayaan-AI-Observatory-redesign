Interpretation

Abu Dhabi's population is projected to reach 4.95 million by 2030, representing an increase of 11.5% (+510K people) from 2025. The population is expected to grow at an average annual rate of approximately 2.2%, supported by continued economic development, workforce demand and ongoing infrastructure investment.

Key Impacts
  • Continued demand for housing, education, healthcare and public services.
  • A growing working-age population supports economic growth and workforce capacity.
  • Continued population growth reinforces the importance of regional infrastructure and service planning.
